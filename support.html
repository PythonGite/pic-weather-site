App Information

App name: PIC Weather Ops

Purpose: operational weather, terrain, fire, and mission-risk decision support for UAS and drone operations.

Important Notice

PIC Weather Ops is a decision-support tool. It is not a certified aviation weather source, an airspace authorization tool, or a replacement for official weather sources, NOTAMs, ATC, UTM, or applicable aviation authority requirements. The operator/RPIC remains responsible for final operational and flight safety decisions before every mission.
