Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026

PIC Weather is an operational weather and mission-support application for drone pilots.

Data Collection

PIC Weather may use the device location to provide weather, mission, map, and operational risk information. Location access is used only to support the app's core functionality, including current-location weather, mission point selection, map tools, and operational briefings.

The app does not require users to create an account.

Weather Data

The app may connect to external weather data providers to retrieve forecasts, wind, gusts, precipitation, visibility, humidity, pressure, sunrise, sunset, and other environmental information.

Third-Party Services

PIC Weather may use third-party weather or map data services to provide weather and location-based information. These services may receive location coordinates or selected place information as needed to return weather or map data.

Stored Data

PIC Weather may store mission points, selected locations, alert preferences, checklist status, and app settings locally on the user's device.

Locally stored data is used to support the app experience and is not sold.

Tracking and Advertising

PIC Weather does not use third-party advertising tracking and does not sell user data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally on the device can be removed by deleting the app or clearing app data where supported by the device or operating system.

Important Notice

PIC Weather supports operational planning. The pilot remains responsible for checking official weather sources, local aviation regulations, airspace restrictions, NOTAMs, and final flight safety conditions before every mission.
